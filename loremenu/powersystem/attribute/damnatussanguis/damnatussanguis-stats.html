COMBAT PASSIVES

Non-Protagonist Logic

100% chance of refusing death on fatal cast. Joker exists as an anomaly; reality cannot delete him without causing a collapse of the current plane.

Grim Reaper Luck

50% chance to negate incoming damage from Material or Light sources. This reflects his status as being "between worlds."

Dread Presence

The ancient weight of his consciousness reduces the speed and magic effectiveness of all non-descendant enemies within a 10-meter radius.

Material Transcendence

Joker does not require sleep or sustenance to maintain his stats. Resting only serves to deepen his connection to the void.
