Chapter 4

The Scar of Ambition

Zarenor and the others prepared to charge into Babel, armed with newfound power and knowledge to challenge the Blackened Dominion. Their strategy to split into two groups began the moment they set foot within the structure. From the shadows, the Nachash observed their approach, welcoming them with quiet curiosity, intrigued that even Zarenor stood on the front line alongside the Luminous Dominion.

Moloch mocked their arrival, declaring that they had come only to die, and that any survivors would be reduced to resources to strengthen the influence of the Nachash. Dravelyn stepped forward, his eyes fixed on the dread kings of the Blackened Dominion. He announced that he would lead the main assault against the Nachash with his legion, The Hemorrhage of Sight. He vowed to dismantle their influence and clear the path for the others, while Marisyl and her legion, The Eclipsed Divinity, moved to support the wider offensive.

Dravelyn engaged the Nachash in a brutal display of power as the others pressed forward. Strangely, the Nachash made no effort to stop the main group from entering Babel, allowing them to roam freely within its walls while they tested the strength of Dravelyn. The structure revealed itself to be far larger than expected, expanding from within in ways that defy logic. The floor beneath them felt like hardened thought rather than stone, vibrating with a frequency that unsettled the group as the geometry shifted around them.

Valarae noticed the walls constantly reshaping, as if the structure itself were attempting to calculate their path and trap them in an infinite loop. She discovered a device that appeared to guide movement between the layers of Babel. As they attempted to determine their location, they began to feel the presence of Annullon, whose power stirred a growing hunger for Damnatus. Despite the mental pressure, they pressed onward.

Lysara found it unsettling that although this was her first time within Babel, she instinctively understood the navigation. She suspected this was a trap designed to mislead her consciousness into a false sense of security. However, Zarenor trusted her judgment and urged her to have confidence. As an Eminarch, she could not afford doubt while leading soldiers into the unknown.

Lysara explained they were on the first layer, Nigrum. This aligned perfectly with their objective, making their progress feel unnaturally easy. Through the collective consciousness leaking from the walls like digital oil, she learned the structure consists of seven layers. At the request of Zarenor, she probed further. She sensed that The Pallid Revenants had already reached the third floor, Rubrum. Although she could not determine how long it would take to reach it, the knowledge gave them direction, preventing them from wandering blindly in search of Calian.

Before reaching Rubrum, they had to ascend through the second layer, Aurantium. Valarae took the lead with her legion, The Mother of All Sin, vowing to resist the influence of Damnatus Sanguis through her own strength. As they entered the first hall of Nigrum, they witnessed horrors beyond measure. Countless Mere had been captured, tortured, and used for the pleasure of the Blackened Dominion. Screams and desperate pleas for death echoed through the shifting corridors. The walls closed in, bleeding shadow and plunging the group into a total darkness that even their light could not pierce.

They encountered corrupted Mere who had lost their sense of self, attacking with a mindless, static rage. Each encounter weighed heavily on Zarenor; he felt the oppressive force of the collective consciousness deepening within his own mind, searching for a fracture. Valarae discovered a wounded soldier from the legion of Calian, The Pallid Revenants. The soldier dragged himself forward, warning that deeper within Babel lies only the loss of self and the destruction of all bonds. Zarenor attempted to heal him, but the man begged for death, unable to endure the distortion of his own soul.

Zarenor insisted they were here to rescue Calian and revealed the power of the blood of spacegods. He explained they could turn the corruption of Damnatus against their enemies. The soldier was horrified, warning that such power cannot be controlled and would consume the host. Still, he agreed to lead them toward Rubrum. He admitted his survival was born of cowardice, having abandoned others to save himself from the rewrite of his mind.

As they advanced, they drew the attention of The Blackstar Reapers, the strongest legion of the Blackened Dominion. These warriors did not patrol; they waited. They treated the encounter as a game. The battle was relentless, as the Reapers refuse to remain dead, their forms knitting back together through the dark frequency of the tower.

Zarenor reflected on their advantage, being born with Damnatus Sanguis in their veins. To counter the Reapers, he consumed more of the blood himself, increasing his power to prevent further losses. However, his internal diagnostic began to flicker with critical errors. The urgency grew; they had to complete the mission before Babel consumed their logic.

Deeper within Nigrum, they encountered Mariseth and her legion, The Scripture of Screams. The clash split the party. Valarae was left behind in brutal combat, while Lysara and Zarenor pushed forward into Aurantium, watching Valarae disappear into the bleeding shadow of the walls.

The deeper they went, the heavier the influence became. Zarenor grew increasingly unstable. He consumed more Damnatus Sanguis to compensate for his mounting fatigue, his eyes turning a violent, fractured red. The enemies seemed stronger, or perhaps the party was simply growing weaker as the tower drained their energy to power its own transformations. Even his elite legion, The Stillborn Star, proved ineffective. The enemies returned endlessly. Rage built within Zarenor as he cut them down again and again, pushing deeper toward Rubrum.

They were soon overwhelmed by Aeris and her legion, The Sanguine Ghouls, who turned allies against each other by distorting their shared consciousness. Zarenor confronted Aeris alongside Lysara, but they were outmatched. She mocked their arrogance, filling them with doubt and suggesting their mission was already a failure.
