Chapter 5

The Godthrone's Gambit

The air in Rubrum did not just hum; it screamed with the frequency of a dying world. Kaelen stood amidst the wreckage of reality, his gaze fixed on the man he had once called a prince. He questioned Zarenor, asking if he had truly become so easily swayed that he would betray every soul who believed in and trusted his judgment.

Zarenor did not flinch. He looked upon Kaelen with eyes that had become fractured prisms of dark light. He commanded Kaelen to be silent, dismissing him as nothing more than a hollow pawn shaped by the hands of Roxara. He declared that Kaelen had no right to question an Eminarch, especially now that he had ascended beyond the limits of that title. As he spoke, his wings manifested fully, tearing through the fabric of his armor like jagged glass and casting a shadow of dissonant energy that seemed to drink the light from the room. Zarenor and his legion, The Stillborn Star, moved with a terrifying, singular intent. They surrounded Kaelen, their consciousness bound into a wall of absolute hostility. Zarenor had no interest in further words; he charged.

The clash was immediate and brutal. The sound was not of steel meeting steel, but of two opposing laws of physics colliding. Kaelen stood his ground, moving with a fluid, lethal grace that defied the logic of the tower. He cut down every soldier that lunged toward him, his blade moving in arcs of perfect precision. One by one they fell, their forms dissolving into golden sparks as Kaelen deleted their presence from the network. Through the chaos, Zarenor advanced, closing the distance until their blades collided with a force that sent a seismic ripple through the collective consciousness of every Mere in Babel.

Amidst the carnage, the surviving forces of Lysara and her legion, The Preceded Light, recognized the horrific truth. This was not a skirmish; it was a cosmic execution. They recognized a fight they could not win. They retreated. Using the violent clash between Zarenor and Kaelen as cover, they began their desperate escape toward the relative safety of Aurantium. Several members of The Stillborn Star attempted to pursue the fleeing light, but each time Kaelen intercepted them. He moved like a ghost through the battlefield, cutting them down while still holding his ground against the unrelenting fury of Zarenor. He protected their escape without faltering, a single man standing against a tide of corruption.

Despite the dark power Zarenor had gained through his betrayal and his unholy allegiance to Zosaius, he could not overcome the anomaly that was Kaelen. Within the pressurized environment of Babel, the presence of Damnatus Sanguis within the veins of Kaelen and his unique link to the collective consciousness amplified his strength beyond all expectation. Each strike landed with the weight of a falling star. Each clash turned in his favor. Zarenor began to understand the terrifying reality: Kaelen was not a pawn; he was a threat to the very existence of the new order. Enraged and desperate, Zarenor pressed harder, his movements becoming a blur of crimson shadow, but the outcome did not change. Wounded and outmatched by a being he considered beneath him, Zarenor cursed Kaelen and retreated from the battle, sacrificing a portion of The Stillborn Star to slow the advance of the agent.

Kaelen won the clash but found no victory in it. He did not linger. He immediately moved to rejoin The Preceded Light, his mind racing with the fear that Zarenor had already reached them. Upon returning to Aurantium, he found a scene of absolute betrayal. They were already engaged in battle against their former allies who had turned under the weight of the tower. The Mother of All Sin no longer stood with them. Kaelen stepped in without hesitation, defending the survivors as they fought back against the combined might of both The Mother of All Sin and The Stillborn Star.

They pushed forward toward the exit, their path paved with the remains of their own kin. As they advanced, they witnessed Valarae, now fully turned and consumed by the void, clashing with Aeris and her legion, The Sanguine Ghouls. Valarae moved with relentless, unnatural speed, cutting through her former allies while laughing in a state of manic bliss driven by the raw power flooding her consciousness. She called out to Aeris, mocking her and asking where her legendary strength and fearlessness had gone now that they were within the walls of a god.

As she continued her assault, Valarae noticed Kaelen and The Preceded Light attempting to escape the trap. She positioned herself in their path, her presence a wall of cold fire, and attempted to persuade them to join Zosaius. She promised them power beyond their mortal limits, a chance to transcend the dying world of the Mere. Before she could strike them down, Aeris ambushed her from behind. The blade of the ghoul drove deep into her back, a strike born of pure spite. Aeris warned Valarae that her newfound power had blinded her and reminded her that they were still within the belly of Babel, where no one is truly safe. Without hesitation, Aeris unleashed her strongest magic at point blank range, a blast of sanguine energy that threw both of them across the hall.

Kaelen took this moment of chaos to urge The Preceded Light to escape Babel before the doors sealed forever. He remained behind, positioning himself as bait while holding his ground against the two warring factions. Drawing upon the collective consciousness of Babel and the volatile power of Damnatus Sanguis, Kaelen struck. He caught Aeris off guard and drove his blade through her chest, draining her very essence for himself. Shock flashed across her face for a final, brief moment before Kaelen beheaded her, ending her existence completely.

Her legion, The Sanguine Ghouls, erupted in a primal rage and charged him. Kaelen cut them down effortlessly, treating them as nothing more than physical obstacles as he advanced toward Valarae. He asked her if she too chose to follow the path of Zarenor and abandon the Millennium Imperialis. Valarae dismissed the question as a concern of the past and sent The Mother of All Sin against him. The outcome does not change. As their blades clashed, she realized that even as a servant of the Mere, Kaelen was far more dangerous than she had imagined. She questioned how he could possess such strength without even holding the rank of an Eminarch. Kaelen answered plainly: as long as they fight within Babel, victory will always slip from her grasp.

Valarae acknowledged the truth in his words. Babel itself was the problem. Without warning, she vanished into a fold of shadow. Kaelen was left momentarily questioning how she escaped the lock of the tower, but he does not dwell on the mystery. He continued forward, cutting down the remaining forces of The Sanguine Ghouls and The Stillborn Star within Aurantium until the halls were silent once more. He arrived at Nigrum soon after, the air thick with the scent of ozone and copper. He encountered several soldiers of The Preceded Light who had remained behind in the fading hope that he survived. They confirmed Lysara was safe and informed him of the current location of Zarenor.

Zarenor now stood before the gates of Babel alongside his new master, Zosaius. Kaelen asked about Dravelyn and his legion, The Hemorrhage of Sight. The soldiers told him that Dravelyn had fled alongside them after the presence of Zosaius was felt by the Nachash. They pleaded with Kaelen, admitting the mission to rescue Calian had failed. They urged him to abandon Babel before the Blackened Dominion unleashed its full wrath upon the survivors. Kaelen reassured them that he would still attempt to save Calian. He believed that the human soul of the man had not been fully consumed by the being.

He left the halls of Babel and stepped into a world of absolute chaos. Zarenor and The Stillborn Star were locked in a desperate battle against The Blackstar Reapers. In the distance, Kaelen saw the Nachash and Zosaius standing amidst the battlefield, engaged in a conversation that seemed to ignore the violence around them. He watched, uncertain of their true purpose. Meanwhile, the Nachash observed as Dravelyn and his legion struggled to hold their ground. Yet the Nachash did not fight back with their usual cruelty. Their focus had shifted. A disturbance spread through their collective consciousness like a ripple on a dark pond. From the third level of Babel, Rubrum, a new power began to emerge, consuming and reshaping their shared awareness.

Before they could comprehend the scale of the rewrite, Zosaius appeared above them. They looked upon him without fear, only with an endless, void like curiosity. Their collective consciousness began to process the impossibility of what stood before them. Zosaius introduced himself as the apex predator of the Mere. The Nachash recognized the truth: Zosaius is not of this world. He comes from Umbra, a realm that exists above the Mundus they sought to conquer.

The Nachash questioned Zosaius, asking what he truly seeks by attempting to eradicate them and force them into his belief. Zosaius repeated his claim with the cold finality of a machine. He is the apex predator, the embodiment of infinite hunger and void consumption. All that exists will be devoured. The Nachash laughed, a sound of dry leaves and static. They did not deny the possibility, yet they do not fear it. Even under attack, they believed that any outcome would serve the Blackened Dominion. Zosaius found this amusing and offered them a place at his side. They refused without a moment of hesitation.

Moloch spoke, his tone filled with contempt. He questioned how a being of such power relies on puppeting a weakened Calian and manipulating Zarenor. To them, this was not enough to threaten the Nachash as a whole. Zosaius declared they would be made to see the truth. They would be transformed and reborn as something eternal in opposition to all Mere. He named this new dominion the Nihilithar Dominion.

The Nachash move through the battlefield with unnatural precision, closing the distance to Zosaius in an instant. All four engaged him at once, their movements perfectly coordinated through their shared consciousness. Even as they are surrounded and attacked from every direction, they deflect and outmaneuver every assault. At the same time, they attempt to siphon power directly from Zosaius, carefully avoiding his blade, knowing that a single strike could strip them of their very identity.

The battle escalated until the ground itself began to weep shadow. The Nachash began to succeed, stealing fragments of his power while wounding him enough to disrupt his hold over the physical vessel of Calian. At that moment, Kaelen entered the clash fully. The battlefield descended into a violent free for all. Kaelen moved between them all, clashing with both sides, his strength amplified by the very tower that sought to crush him. The continuous battle weakened Zosaius, destabilizing his anchor to this plane.

An opening appeared. Kaelen struck. His blade cut into Zosaius while the entity was distracted, landing multiple blows in rapid succession that echoed like thunder. Zosaius halted. He declared that Mundus is now doomed by his will and by the Nihilithar Dominion. The dominion gathered around him, their forms blurring into a single mass of shadow. Above them, the skies of Mundus began to warp and bleed. A massive eye formed across the heavens, vast as the sphere of Mundus itself, casting a shadow that covered all existence below.

Zosaius and his dominion ascended toward the celestial eye. It opened. Its consciousness spread across Mundus, flooding the minds of all Mere with a vision of the end. From within it, colossal serpentine beings began to fall, descending into the world like living spears. The people of Mundus watched in horror as the sky birthed creatures beyond comprehension. Winged serpents, vast as mountains, crashed into the world, shattering the foundations of the cities. An apocalypse had begun.

The Nihilithar Dominion disappeared beyond the eye into the unknown. Before leaving, Zosaius spoke to Kaelen, his voice a curse that etched itself into the bloodline of the agent for all time. Then he was gone. The body he controlled fell from the sky like a broken doll. Kaelen reached out and caught Calian, now freed from the possession. The sky returned to normal, as if the eye had never been there, but the world was forever changed.

The battle was over. Kaelen used the device given by Roxara to return to the Millennium Imperialis. The moment he arrived, his body gave out. The wounds, the exhaustion, and the overwhelming knowledge forced upon him by the collective consciousness finally take their toll. Among that knowledge, one name remains clear: Leviathan.

Roxara remained satisfied. Enough survived to rebuild under her vision. New history will be written. New laws will be established for all Mere, even those who once stood with the Blackened Dominion. A new balance of power emerges, as the Nihilithar Dominion rises as an unknown threat from beyond the skies. Calian and Lysara remain in a state of unconsciousness, their souls scarred by the touch of the Outer God. Calian awakened days later, consumed by the memory of the destruction he caused. He is no longer seen as an Eminarch; he is seen as the catalyst for the end.

Across Mundus, fear spreads like a plague. The Mere now understand that something far greater has begun. Old warnings from Aien return to the surface, prophecies of a being that would descend to bring ruin as a punishment for the defiance of Zephryn. Now, that warning is no longer a distant myth. Mundus has changed. Beings from beyond this plane now walk its surface, and they do not come in peace.
