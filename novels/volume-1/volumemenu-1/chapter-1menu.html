Chapter 1

The First Awakening

After gaining consciousness and awakening within the Mundus, the center of the Godthrone continent, Zephryn was not the only of the first Mere to awaken. Alongside him arose Toxara, another of the Mere. Together, they grew to know one another, exploring the vast expanse of the Mundus and deciding to rule it.

By sexually binding their consciousness, Zephryn and Toxara discovered how to initiate a population, kickstarting the Mere. This act granted Toxara the ability to give birth without aid, though she cherished pregnancy and held onto their first child, Siara, as a potential safeguard for the future. Nevertheless, Toxara used her newfound power to manually awaken other Mere, not naturally as powerful as herself or Zephryn, but created in their image. In this way, a family tree of descendants of Zephryn was born, later known as the Benevolent Tyrant, or Father of Mere.

Those of descendant or noble blood inherited innate perks tied to Zephryn, though every Mere was made in his image. Those whom Zephryn had directly bonded with were naturally stronger nobles compared to those awakened solely by Toxara. Even so, all Mere, whether descendants of Zephryn or created by Toxara, sought to follow Zephryn’s example, birthing their own Mere and building families within the Mundus, aiding Zephryn in shaping nations.

Giving rise to the capital of the Mundus and home to the Mere.

At the very center of Mundus, where the light of the sun kisses the world, rises Solara, city of kings, sorcery, and destiny. Founded by Zephryn, it is more than a capital; it is the living pulse of collective consciousness, a place where the energy of Zephryn flows like molten gold through streets, spires, and temples.

Being tied to Zephryn or Toxara conferred additional senses and abilities. Many Mere could go without sleep or sustenance, though these acts remained optional. Any being who indulged in eating or resting could grow stronger over time compared to their siblings, even among the Mere. Made in Zephryn’s image, they could also learn from the collective consciousness, as everything within Godthrone was connected to his consciousness, allowing them to gain and master extraordinary powers.

Through consciousness, Mere could achieve diverse mindsets, abilities, and perspectives, granting limitless potential. The effectiveness of these powers depended on the strength of the individual and the depth of their consciousness within the Godthrone continent.

Zephryn taught his children to harness power through their consciousness and the collective consciousness. Through the essence of Godthrone himself, Zephryn introduced what would be known as Magic among the Mere, sharing knowledge to benefit nations and make life within the Mundus easier. Zephryn watched them struggle, their minds flickering as they tried to pull power from the void, before he showed them how to structure that energy through their own perception.

He taught how to fight the inhabitants beyond the Mere within the Mundus, learning from each creature and understanding the threats they posed. Being made straight from Godthrone’s own consciousness, everything within the Godthrone continent was tied to the collective consciousness.

He demonstrated the construction of fire magic through foresight of consciousness and extraction of power from the collective consciousness. He advanced further by combining lightning magic, creating unique manifestations, a lightning bolt imbued with fire’s heat, shaped and cast through his own perception.

Zephryn also taught the fundamentals of Magic in crafting anima. Anima are a manifestation of one’s own consciousness, taking the form of a weapon or tool bonded to a name and attuned to the individual. Zephryn felt the strain of this process as he demonstrated it for the first time, his own consciousness thinning as he forced it into a rigid, physical state. It was a trade of essence for utility. Stronger Mere, and those closer in blood to Zephryn, could manifest these tools from nothing, while others required guidance or an existing focus to awaken their internal strength.

As the Mere grew stronger and built their nations, a quiet restlessness took hold. They had mapped the Mundus and mastered its laws. Aware that existence was vast and that the Godthrone continent held more than just their origin plane, they grew bored. They sought to test their limits against the unknown. Zephryn felt the collective itch of his children, a dull pressure in his mind that refused to be silenced by the peace of Solara. Staying within the Mundus would lead to stagnation.

Zephryn gathered his strongest Mere and traveled to the edge of the Mundus. He stood where the familiar golden pulse of the collective consciousness met the absolute silence of the void. The sheer nothingness beyond the edge pressed against him like a physical wall. Zephryn did not just walk forward; he pushed his entire consciousness into his palm, focusing every ounce of his authority into a single point. He struck the void.

The fabric of reality shrieked, resisting his will with a cold, abrasive force that burned against his mind. For a heartbeat, the Mundus fought back, trying to delete the anomaly he was creating. Zephryn poured more of his essence into the strike, refusing to be contained. With a sound like tearing silk, the void gave way. A jagged rift opened, a scar linking the Mundus to the next plane, bleeding raw light into the darkness. He had created a path for all who followed.
