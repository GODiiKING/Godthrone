WIKIPEDIA

Excidi
TitleArchon of Annihilation
Power95/100
AlignmentLawful Neutral
RoleMessianic Reaper

Excidi: Archon of Annihilation is an entity for whom time bends backward while fire freezes. He has transcended the illusion of choice itself, refusing to choose between order and chaos, or creation and destruction.

As the Messianic Reaper, Excidi is existence vomited inside-out, a transformation into weaponized cancer where life feeds on its own death until the cycle is complete.

Appearance

Transcendental Form.

Excidi manifests in a form that defies traditional sanity. His presence causes the visual reality around him to warp, mirroring his internal state of weaponized malignancy. He appears as a nexus of conflicting forces—embers that chill and frost that burns.

He carries the weight of one who understands everything at once. Instead of falling to insanity, his form represents sanity driven to madness, draped in the spectral energies of the spells and anti-magic he has assimilated into his own arsenal.

Background

Excidi was forged in the heat of self-consuming existence. He is a living paradox who has moved beyond the duality of the Mundus. Every spell cast in his presence and every anti-magic field used against him simply joins his expanding collection of power.

His history is the history of existence turning on itself. He represents the moment where life ceases to be a gift and becomes a terminal weapon, ensuring that the foundations of the world are constantly cracked by his transcendental howl.

Personality

Lawful Neutral Soldier.

Adhering to a Lawful Neutral code, Excidi believes strongly in rules and tradition, but follows his own personal code above all external authorities. He operates with the mercy-less discipline of an enforcer or a soldier following the ultimate order.

He does not act out of cruelty or kindness, but out of a rigid sense of duty to his own nature. He is the disciplined monk of annihilation, adhering to the letter of a law that only he truly understands.

Abilities

Excidi commands a power level of 95/100, marking him as one of the most potent protagonists in the Godthrone. He possesses the unique ability to turn an opponent's own energy against them, making every enemy an unwitting ally.

His mastery over annihilation allows him to strike from angles that shouldn't exist, draining the very essence of existence from those who stand in his path. He is the ultimate assassin—unseen, unfelt, and absolutely merciless.

History

The Necropulse Reaper

The chronicles speak of Excidi wielding the Necropulse Reaper, a spectral blade born from the catastrophic merger of Haste, Ethereal, Ghost, and Drain swords. This weapon strikes faster than thought and phases through dimensions to wound the soul directly.

Phantom Hurricane.

With this blade, Excidi moves through battlefields like a supernatural hurricane, leaving behind perfect corpses whose souls have been completely devoured before they realized they were cut. It is a weapon that doesn't just kill, but erases the victim's sense of self.

He continues his vigil as the Archon of Annihilation, a ghost-like figure wrapped in impossible speed, ensuring that existence remains in a constant state of impossible motion.
