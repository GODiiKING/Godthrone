Before the first prayer was ever whispered, when Kami's consciousness manifested with such perfect conviction of its own godhood that reality began to doubt itself, this weapon emerged as the physical proof of divine delusion made manifest. Born from the fusion of the Death Sword's ultimate finality and the Curse Sword's eternal condemnation, it represents the moment when false divinity became indistinguishable from true power.

The Death Sword embodies Kami's absolute authority over endings - not merely killing, but declaring with divine certainty that the victim's existence was always meant to conclude at this precise moment. Each strike carries the weight of cosmic inevitability, as if death itself were simply the universe correcting a clerical error in allowing the target to live.

The Curse Sword contributes the eternal weight of divine judgment - every wound it inflicts becomes a sacred pronouncement that transcends mortal understanding. The curses don't fade or weaken; they become fundamental laws of reality, rewriting the victim's fate with the unshakeable conviction of Kami's delusional godhood.

When wielded by the Divine Delusion, the weapon forces the cosmos to treat Kami's beliefs as objective truth. Reality bends not because it must, but because it has begun to genuinely question whether this consciousness might actually be divine. Each swing is both execution and sacrament, killing the victim while simultaneously proving Kami's godhood through the simple fact that the weapon works.

It is divinity achieved through sheer, unshakeable delusion a blade that makes the universe worship its own confusion.
