WIKIPEDIA

Khaos
TitleEater of Names
Threat77/100
NovelMythos of Creation
RoleIncorporeal Dark

Khaos: Eater of Names is a shadowy incorporeal entity dwelling within a human vessel. He is known by many titles including The Unspeakable, He Who is Not to be Named, and the Lord of Interstellar Spaces. He embodies the pure concept of nothingness.

Background

The Waiting Dark.

He was once a man, but he is no longer. He has become a waiting dark, a presence that exists between the stars. As the Eater of Names, his history is one of consumption, where identity itself is dissolved into his void.

He functions as the Mythos of Creation, representing the absolute silence and absence that preceded the first light of the world cycles.

Personality

The Void Individualist.

Chaos follows his own heart, shirking the rules and traditions of the Mundus. He is an individualist who promotes the ideals of freedom above all else, though it is his own freedom that always comes first.

To Chaos, the concepts of good and evil are secondary to his need to remain unbound. He moves through existence with a clinical detachment from the protocols of the High Gods.

Appearance

Lord of Interstellar Spaces.

Though he occupies a human body, his true form is an incorporeal shadow that occasionally bleeds through the physical shell. He appears as a living void, absorbing the light around him.

His presence is marked by a profound silence, and those who look too closely into his vessel often report seeing the infinite emptiness of interstellar space staring back at them.

Abilities

The Blackened Silence.

Khaos has mastered the Rule of Threes, weaving together Time, Space, and Shadow into the art of Blackened Silence. He can see through the comfortable lies of existence using his Abysmal Sight.

As a being of a higher dimension, he can consume magic to fuel his arcane techniques, manifesting artifacts like the Thousand Faced Darkness to devour eternity itself.

History

The Formation of the Obscenities

Forged in the moment when reality first learned to fear itself, Khaos birthed the Twin Obscenities. These blades, Whisper and Hunger, were born from the unholy fusion of four cursed weapons that violated the rules of warfare.

He used these horrors to carve a path through the ancient era, turning combat into a feeding frenzy that offended existence itself. He remains an anomaly that reality cannot delete without risking total collapse.

Feeding the Void.

He continues to dwell in the interstellar spaces, waiting for the moments when the Mundus becomes fragile enough for the nothingness to reclaim what was once taken.
