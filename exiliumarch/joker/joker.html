Joker is an Ancient and Cursed being from the forgotten ages, where the veils between gods and mortals were thinner.Than the blood in one's veins, there arose a warrior who defied the very essence of mortality. Not a champion of any-cause, nor a savior of any realms.

When civilizations were as nothing more than whispers in the dust. Realms both in the living and the dead. Slipping between-worlds as one might walk

A being who transcends the boundaries of both life and death. Not crafted by mortal hands, nor brought into existence through-divine means.
